# Privacy Policy for Claw Companion

Effective date: May 14, 2026

Claw Companion is an iOS companion app for OpenClaw.

## Information We Collect

Claw Companion does not collect, sell, or share personal information with third parties.

The app may store gateway connection details, device identifiers, access tokens, private keys, or other credentials locally on your device so that it can connect to your OpenClaw gateway. These values are used only to provide app functionality.

## OpenClaw Gateway Data

When you connect Claw Companion to an OpenClaw gateway, the app may display data provided by that gateway, including sessions, messages, scheduled tasks, memory entries, usage information, agent activity, and related metadata.

This data is exchanged directly between your device and the OpenClaw gateway you configure. Claw Companion does not operate a separate cloud service to collect or store this information.

## Local Storage

Claw Companion may store app settings, gateway configuration, and credentials locally on your device. Sensitive values are stored using iOS secure storage where supported.

## Third-Party Services

Claw Companion does not include third-party advertising or analytics services.

If your OpenClaw gateway is configured to use third-party AI providers, APIs, infrastructure tools, or other services, data sent to those services is handled according to your gateway configuration and those services' privacy policies.

## Data Sharing

Claw Companion does not sell user data.

Claw Companion does not share user data with advertisers or data brokers.

## Children's Privacy

Claw Companion is not intended for children.

## Open Source

Claw Companion may be distributed as open-source software. Users and contributors can review the app's source code in the project repository.
